# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Citizen Services and Cyber Vigilance
**Circular No.:** CIR-2026-09

## 1 Background
Multiple complaints have been reported across districts of Mitrapur Pradesh regarding unauthorized individuals impersonating government officials and contacting welfare scheme applicants through phone calls, SMS, and messaging apps. These callers falsely claim that approved scheme benefits are pending release and attempt to extort money or compromise citizen bank accounts.

## 2 Amended Clause
All citizens, beneficiaries, and field facilitation staff are advised to adhere strictly to the following public safety directive:

- **Warning Against Fake Calls:** Citizens are strongly warned against fake **"scheme money release"** calls demanding a fee of **₹500** (or any other amount) or requesting **OTPs** (One-Time Passwords), UPI PINs, or bank card details to credit welfare grants.
- **Official Disbursement Policy:** The Government of Mitrapur Pradesh does **not** charge ₹500 or any release fee, and no government official will ever ask for an OTP over the phone. All approved welfare funds are transferred automatically via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- **Reporting Fraud:** Citizens receiving such calls should disconnect immediately, avoid sharing OTPs or transferring funds, and report the caller number to the nearest Jan Seva Kendra, local police station, or the National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930).

## 3 Effective Date
This public alert circular takes effect on **2026-06-01** and remains in continuous effect until further notice.
