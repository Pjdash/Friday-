# Shramik Suraksha Bima (SCH-INS-01)

- **English:** Shramik Suraksha Bima
- **Hindi (Devanagari):** श्रमिक सुरक्षा बीमा
- **Kannada:** ಶ್ರಮಿಕ ಸುರಕ್ಷಾ ವಿಮೆ
- **Tamil:** தொழிலாளர் பாதுகாப்பு காப்பீடு
- **Implementing Department:** Department of Labour & Informal Sector Welfare, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh

## 1 Purpose

Shramik Suraksha Bima delivers state-funded personal accident insurance protection to informal daily-wage earners and farmers in Mitrapur Pradesh. The scheme shields working families against sudden economic distress caused by accidental death or permanent total disability of an earning member.

## 2 Benefit

- **Cover Amount:** ₹2,00,000 personal accident cover.
- **Payout Trigger:**
  - **Accidental Death:** ₹2,00,000 lump sum paid via DBT to the registered nominee's verified bank account.
  - **Permanent Total Disability due to Accident:** ₹2,00,000 lump sum paid via DBT directly to the enrolled beneficiary.
- **Beneficiary Premium:** ₹0 (the entire insurance premium is borne by the Government of Mitrapur Pradesh).

## 3 Eligibility

An applicant must satisfy all of the following conditions:

- **Residency:** Permanent or working resident of Mitrapur Pradesh.
- **Occupation:** `occupation` must be `daily_wage` OR `farmer` (`occupation in {daily_wage, farmer}`).
- **Age:** `18 <= age <= 59` years (applicant must be at least 18 years old and not older than 59 years as on the date of enrolment).

## 4 Required Documents

- **Mitrapur Resident Identity Card** — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Must be active and unrevoked.
- **Age Proof Document** (Birth Certificate, School Certificate, or Government Photo ID showing date of birth) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Permanent validity once issued by a competent authority.
- **Occupation Proof** (Labour Facilitation Card / Employer Work Certificate for `daily_wage` workers, OR Mitrapur Bhoomi Record / Gram Panchayat Cultivator Certificate for `farmer`) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Labour card must be active and renewed within the past 24 months; land or Gram Panchayat certificate must be issued within the last 12 months.
- **Nominee Declaration & Nominee Identity Copy** — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Nominee ID must be active; nomination remains valid until updated by the beneficiary.
- **Bank Account Proof** (beneficiary and nominee bank details) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Accounts must be active and DBT-enabled.
- **Mobile Number Verification Record** — **Optional.** *Validity note:* Used solely for policy certificate SMS alerts; never share OTPs with third parties (see `POL-FEE-01`).

## 5 Application Process

1. Enrol online via the **MitraSeva Citizen Portal** or at any **MitraKendra Facilitation Centre** or **Block Labour Welfare Office**.
2. Submit occupation, age, and nominee details to receive an **Application Reference Number (ARN)**.
3. The Block Labour Inspector verifies occupation status and age eligibility within 14 working days; once approved, a digital Policy Enrolment Certificate is issued.
4. In the event of an insured accident, the beneficiary or nominee submits the claim along with the Police FIR / Medical Disability Assessment within 90 days of the incident.
5. **Final Decision:** Portal enrolment receipts and inspector checks are preliminary. The **Department of Labour & Informal Sector Welfare makes the final decision** on enrolment eligibility, policy issuance, and accident claim settlement.
6. Enrolment and claim settlement carry zero fees (see `POL-FEE-01`).

## 6 Last Date and Validity

- **Status:** Open (continuous enrolment).
- **Effective From:** `2026-04-01`
- **Last Date to Apply:** None (no closing date specified under this guideline).
- **Validity:** Remains open until amended, suspended, or closed by an official circular issued by the Department of Labour & Informal Sector Welfare.
