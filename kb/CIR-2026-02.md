# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of School Education
**Order / Amendment No.:** CIR-2026-02

## 1 Background
Representations have been received from district education officers, school principals, and parents requesting additional time to complete bonafide certificate issuance and online portal submissions for the **Balika Vidya Scholarship** (`SCH-EDU-01`) for the current cycle. To ensure that no eligible girl student is excluded due to administrative delays, the State Government has approved a one-time extension of the application window.

## 2 Amended Clause
The application deadline clause for the **Balika Vidya Scholarship** (`SCH-EDU-01`) is hereby amended as follows:

- **Existing `apply_until` Deadline:** 2026-10-31
- **Amended `apply_until` Deadline:** **2026-11-21**

All other eligibility criteria, scholarship amounts, and document requirements under `SCH-EDU-01` remain unchanged.

## 3 Effective Date
This amendment takes effect on **2026-10-25**. Eligible applicants may submit completed applications through the Mitrapur Pradesh Scholarship Portal or authorized Jan Seva Kendras up to **2026-11-21**.
