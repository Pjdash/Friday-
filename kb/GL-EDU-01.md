# Balika Vidya Scholarship (SCH-EDU-01)

- **English:** Balika Vidya Scholarship
- **Hindi (Devanagari):** बालिका विद्या छात्रवृत्ति
- **Kannada:** ಬಾಲಿಕಾ ವಿದ್ಯಾ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ
- **Tamil:** பாலிகா வித்யா கல்வி உதவித்தொகை
- **Implementing Department:** Directorate of School & Higher Education, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh

## 1 Purpose

The Balika Vidya Scholarship supports the continued secondary, senior secondary, and undergraduate education of female students from low- and moderate-income households in Mitrapur Pradesh. By offsetting tuition, textbook, and examination expenses, the scheme works to reduce dropout rates among young women.

## 2 Benefit

- **Amount:** ₹10,000 per academic year.
- **Disbursement Mode:** Credited as a single annual lump sum via DBT to the student's verified bank account (or joint account with a parent/legal guardian if the student is a minor).
- **Renewal:** Eligible students may reapply each academic year provided all eligibility conditions remain satisfied.

## 3 Eligibility

An applicant must meet all of the following conditions:

- **Gender:** `gender = female`.
- **Occupation:** `occupation = student` (currently enrolled full-time in a recognised school, junior college, polytechnic, or degree college in Mitrapur Pradesh).
- **Age:** `age <= 22` years as on the date of application.
- **Household Income:** `annual_household_income <= ₹2,50,000` (combined annual income of parents/guardians).

## 4 Required Documents

- **Mitrapur Resident Identity Card** (student or parent/guardian if minor) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Must be active and unrevoked.
- **Bonafide Student Enrolment Certificate** (issued by the Head of Institution / Principal) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Must be issued specifically for the 2026–27 academic year and dated within 90 days of application.
- **Age Proof Document** (Birth Certificate or School Registration Record) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Permanent validity once issued by a recognised authority.
- **Annual Household Income Certificate** (issued by the Block Revenue Officer / Tehsil Office) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Valid for 12 months from the date of issue.
- **Bank Account Proof** (student's single or minor-joint account passbook) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Account must be active and DBT-enabled.
- **Previous Academic Year Marksheet** — **Optional.** *Validity note:* Helpful for renewal tracking; must be certified by the previous institution if submitted.

## 5 Application Process

1. Complete the online scholarship form on the **MitraSeva Citizen Portal** or submit documents at the school/college **Scholarship Nodal Desk** or nearest **MitraKendra**.
2. The enrolled educational institution digitally attests the student's bonafide status within 10 working days of submission.
3. The District Education Scholarship Committee audits income and age records against state registries.
4. **Final Decision:** Institutional attestation confirms enrolment only. The **Directorate of School & Higher Education makes the final decision** on eligibility confirmation, award sanction, and scholarship disbursement.
5. Applying for the scholarship is strictly free of charge (see `POL-FEE-01`).
