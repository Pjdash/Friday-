# Grievance Redressal, Verification & Appeals Policy (POL-GRV-01)

- **Issuing Authority:** Department of Welfare Governance & Citizen Services, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
- **Applicability:** All welfare schemes administered by departments of the Government of Mitrapur Pradesh.

## 1 Scope and Objective

This policy provides a transparent, time-bound mechanism for citizens of Mitrapur Pradesh to contest erroneous application rejections, delayed benefit disbursements, bank transfer failures, or document verification discrepancies across all state welfare schemes.

## 2 Authority for Final Decisions and Grounds for Appeal

- **Departmental Final Authority:** For every welfare scheme, the respective **implementing department makes the final decision** on eligibility verification, sanction, modification, and disbursement. Pre-checks performed by citizen portals, helpdesks, or field facilitators are advisory only.
- **Written Reasons Mandatory:** Whenever an application is rejected or kept on hold, the implementing department must record a specific, plain-language reason linked to the applicant's Application Reference Number (ARN) (e.g., expired income certificate, acreage above threshold, or district outside scheme coverage).
- **Valid Grounds for Appeal:** Applicants may file an appeal if they believe a valid document was misread, an eligibility threshold was misapplied, a DBT transfer failed due to banking error, or an official circular amendment was not reflected in the decision.

## 3 Two-Tier Redressal Process and Timelines

1. **Tier 1 — Block Grievance Desk (within 30 days of rejection/delay):**
   - File a zero-fee grievance online via the **MitraSeva Citizen Portal** or in person at the **Block Welfare Office** quoting the ARN and attaching any corrected/missing valid documents.
   - **Resolution SLA:** The Block Grievance Officer must review and issue a written decision within **15 working days**.
2. **Tier 2 — District Appellate Welfare Committee (within 30 days of Tier 1 order):**
   - If unsatisfied with the Tier 1 decision or if no response is received within 15 working days, the applicant may escalate to the District Appellate Welfare Committee chaired by the Additional District Magistrate (Welfare).
   - **Resolution SLA:** Final appellate order issued within **30 working days**, binding on the district implementing unit.

## 4 Zero-Fee Protection and Anti-Retaliation

- Filing a grievance or appeal is completely free of charge under `POL-FEE-01`. Any demand for payment to expedite an appeal must be reported to the `fraud_desk`.
- If an appeal succeeds and confirms the applicant was eligible on the original application date, pending benefits are released prospectively and, where permitted by scheme rules, with applicable arrears.
