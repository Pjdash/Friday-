# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Agriculture and Revenue
**Circular No.:** CIR-2026-08

## 1 Background
To reduce paperwork, prevent delays caused by damaged or misplaced physical land documents, and align scheme administration with Mitrapur Pradesh's digital land records initiative, the Department has approved the use of electronic land records for beneficiary verification under scheme `SCH-FRM-02`.

## 2 Amended Clause
The acceptable proof-of-land-holding provisions under `SCH-FRM-02` are hereby updated to include the following directive:

- **Accepted Alternative Document:** The official **digital land passbook** issued through the Mitrapur Pradesh Revenue Portal (or verified via DigiLocker with a scannable QR code) is hereby added as an accepted alternative to the traditional **paper patta**.
- **Mandatory Acceptance:** District agriculture offices, verification officers, and Jan Seva Kendras shall treat a valid digital land passbook as legally equivalent to a paper patta and shall not insist on physical paper patta where a verifiable digital land passbook is presented.

## 3 Effective Date
This circular takes effect on **2026-08-15** and applies to all new and ongoing applications under `SCH-FRM-02`.
