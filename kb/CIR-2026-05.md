# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Skill Development and Youth Affairs
**Circular No.:** CIR-2026-05

## 1 Background
To expand industry-aligned vocational training and employment readiness among youth in Mitrapur Pradesh, the State Cabinet has approved the launch of the **Yuva Kaushal Grant** (`SCH-EDU-02`). This circular notifies citizens, training partners, and district facilitation centers of the official launch schedule.

## 2 Amended Clause
The following operational provisions govern the launch of **Yuva Kaushal Grant** (`SCH-EDU-02`):

- **Scheduled Launch Date:** The **Yuva Kaushal Grant** (`SCH-EDU-02`) will officially open for applications on **2026-12-15**.
- **Pre-Launch Restrictions:** No applications, pre-registrations, or fee payments shall be accepted prior to **2026-12-15**. Detailed scheme guidelines and application forms will be activated on the official Mitrapur Pradesh Portal on the launch date.
- **Facilitation Readiness:** All district skill development offices and Jan Seva Kendras are instructed to complete staff orientation before **2026-12-15**.

## 3 Effective Date
The **Yuva Kaushal Grant** (`SCH-EDU-02`) launch and application window take effect on **2026-12-15**.
