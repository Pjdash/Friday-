# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Social Welfare
**Order / Amendment No.:** CIR-2026-11

## 1 Background
During the 2026 annual verification drive for scheme `SCH-PEN-01`, district authorities sought interim guidance on validating applicant income certificates while a comprehensive revision of `GL-PEN-01` Section 3 was under review. This interim amendment provided temporary verification rules until the updated income threshold could be formally notified.

## 2 Amended Clause
Pending revision of `GL-PEN-01` Section 3, the following interim clarification applied to `SCH-PEN-01` applications:

- **Income Certificate Validity:** Income certificates issued by a competent revenue authority (Tehsildar or equivalent) within the preceding 12 months showing an annual household income within the then-applicable ceiling of ₹1,20,000 were accepted as valid proof of income for `SCH-PEN-01`.
- **Superseded Status:** This interim clarification is no longer in force and has been **superseded by `CIR-2026-01`** effective **2026-09-10**. Refer to `CIR-2026-01` for the current income eligibility limit and requirements.

## 3 Effective Date
This interim amendment was effective from **2026-05-01** through **2026-09-09** (`valid_until: 2026-09-09`) and ceased to apply upon the commencement of `CIR-2026-01` on **2026-09-10**.
