# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities
**Order / Amendment No.:** CIR-2026-03

## 1 Background
Based on recommendations of the State Medical Assessment Committee, the Government of Mitrapur Pradesh has decided to broaden coverage under scheme `SCH-DIS-01` by revising the threshold disability percentage specified in guideline `GL-DIS-01`. This amendment is strictly limited to Section 3 of `GL-DIS-01`.

## 2 Amended Clause
**Section 3 of `GL-DIS-01` ONLY** is hereby amended as follows:

- **Existing Provision:** Applicants must have a certified minimum `disability_percent` of **40%**.
- **Amended Provision:** Applicants must have a certified minimum `disability_percent` of **35%**.

**Scope Limitation:** No other section, clause, benefit rate, income ceiling, age requirement, or procedural rule of `GL-DIS-01` is modified by this amendment. All other clauses of `GL-DIS-01` remain in full force without change.

## 3 Effective Date
This amendment takes effect on **2026-11-10** and applies to all applications submitted or assessed on or after **2026-11-10**.
