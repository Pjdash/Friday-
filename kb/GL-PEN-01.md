# Vriddhjan Pension Yojana (SCH-PEN-01)

- **English:** Vriddhjan Pension Yojana
- **Hindi (Devanagari):** वृद्धजन पेंशन योजना
- **Kannada:** ವೃದ್ಧಜನ ಪಿಂಚಣಿ ಯೋಜನೆ
- **Tamil:** முதியோர் ஓய்வூதியத் திட்டம்
- **Implementing Department:** Directorate of Social Security & Senior Welfare, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh

## 1 Purpose

The Vriddhjan Pension Yojana provides steady monthly income support to economically vulnerable senior citizens residing in Mitrapur Pradesh. The scheme aims to safeguard basic living dignity, nutritional needs, and routine healthcare expenses for elderly residents who lack adequate household earnings.

## 2 Benefit

- **Amount:** ₹1,500 per month.
- **Disbursement Mode:** Credited directly to the beneficiary's verified single-holder bank account via the Mitrapur Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) gateway by the 7th working day of each month.
- **Continuity:** Continued monthly subject to annual life-verification renewal at a MitraKendra Facilitation Centre or through the MitraSeva Portal.

## 3 Eligibility

An applicant must satisfy all of the following conditions:

- **Residency:** Permanent resident of Mitrapur Pradesh.
- **Age:** `age >= 60` years as on the date of application.
- **Household Income:** `annual_household_income <= ₹1,20,000` (combined annual income of all earning members in the household).
- **Exclusions:** Applicants currently receiving a regular government employee superannuation pension from Mitrapur Pradesh are ineligible.

## 4 Required Documents

- **Mitrapur Resident Identity Card** — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Must be active and unrevoked; address must reflect current residence in Mitrapur Pradesh.
- **Age Proof Certificate** (Birth Certificate, School Leaving Certificate, or Medical Age Assessment from a District Government Hospital) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Permanent validity once issued by a competent authority.
- **Annual Household Income Certificate** (issued by the Block Revenue Officer / Tehsil Office) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Valid for 12 months from the date of issue; must be unexpired on the date of application.
- **Bank Passbook Front Page / Cancelled Cheque** (single-holder account in the applicant's name) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Account must be active and DBT-enabled; passbook entry must be updated within the last 6 months.
- **Recent Passport-Size Photograph** — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Taken within the last 6 months.
- **Ration Card / Family Registry Extract** — **Optional.** *Validity note:* Accepted as supplementary household composition proof if valid for the current calendar year.

## 5 Application Process

1. Submit an application online via the **MitraSeva Citizen Portal** or in person at the nearest **MitraKendra Facilitation Centre** or **Block Welfare Office (BWO)**.
2. Upload or present clear copies of all mandatory documents and receive a stamped or digital **Application Reference Number (ARN)**.
3. Field verification of age, residency, and household income is conducted by the Block Social Security Inspector within 21 working days.
4. **Final Decision:** Portal pre-checks and MitraKendra acknowledgements are preliminary only. The **Directorate of Social Security & Senior Welfare makes the final decision** on eligibility verification, sanction, modification, or rejection of every application.
5. No fee is charged at any stage of application or benefit release (see `POL-FEE-01`).

## 6 Last Date and Validity

- **Status:** Open (rolling admissions throughout the year).
- **Effective From:** `2026-04-01`
- **Last Date to Apply:** None (no end date).
- **Validity:** Remains in force indefinitely unless amended, superseded, or closed by an official circular issued by the Directorate of Social Security & Senior Welfare.
