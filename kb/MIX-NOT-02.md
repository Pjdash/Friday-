# Jan Seva Kendra Appointment Booking - Hinglish Guide

**Q1. Jan Seva Kendra visit ke liye appointment kab book karna chahiye?**
Aapko visit se kam se kam **24 ghante (24-hour lead time) pehle** appointment book karna zaroori hai. Same-day walk-in mein lambi line ya slot na milne ki dikkat ho sakti hai.

**Q2. Kya Saturday ko bhi Jan Seva Kendra khula rehta hai?**
Haan. `CIR-2026-10` (effective `2026-05-15`) ke mutabik **Nayagaon**, **Dakshinpur**, aur **Thendralnagar** ke sabhi Jan Seva Kendras Saturday ko extended hours **10:00 se 14:00** tak khule rehte hain.

**Q3. Appointment booking ya scheme application ki kitni fees lagti hai?**
Scheme application aur benefit release ke liye government fee **₹0** hai (`POL-FEE-01`). Kisi agent ya middleman ko extra paisa na dein.

**Q4. Kendra jaate waqt kya saath le jaayein?**
Apne saare **original documents** (Aadhaar, income certificate, bank passbook, land record ya bonafide certificate — scheme ke hisaab se) aur appointment slip saath rakhein.

**Q5. Kya phone par OTP batana zaroori hota hai?**
Nahi. Kisi unknown caller ko kabhi apna **OTP** na batayein aur na hi ₹500 jaisi fake fee dein (`CIR-2026-09`). Aisi maang par **fraud_desk** ko report karein.

**Q6. Kya Jan Seva Kendra par form submit hote hi scheme approve ho jaati hai?**
Nahi. Appointment book karne ya document jama karne se automatic approval nahi milta. Verification ke baad sambandhit government department hi final decision leta hai.
