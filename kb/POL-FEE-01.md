# Zero-Fee & Anti-Fraud Policy (POL-FEE-01)

- **Issuing Authority:** Department of Welfare Governance & Citizen Services, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
- **Applicability:** All welfare schemes, scholarships, subsidies, pensions, grants, and insurance programmes administered by the Government of Mitrapur Pradesh.

## 1 Scope and Objective

This policy establishes a strict zero-fee citizen service guarantee across all welfare schemes of Mitrapur Pradesh and protects applicants from unauthorized intermediaries, bribery, impersonation, and digital fraud. Every department, district office, Gram Panchayat desk, and MitraKendra Facilitation Centre is legally bound by these provisions.

## 2 Zero-Fee Guarantee, OTP Safety, and Fraud Reporting

- **No Fee for Application or Benefit Release:** No fee, commission, service charge, processing levy, or "speed-up" payment is charged for any scheme application, eligibility check, field inspection, document verification, sanction letter, or benefit release under any Mitrapur Pradesh welfare scheme.
- **Never Share OTPs or Pay Middlemen:** Citizens must **never share One-Time Passwords (OTPs)**, bank PINs, net-banking passwords, or ATM CVV numbers with any caller, agent, or unofficial intermediary. No government official will ever ask for a citizen's bank PIN or demand money to "unlock" or "release" a DBT transfer. Do not pay middlemen or brokers; official assistance at MitraKendra Facilitation Centres and Block Welfare Offices is completely free.
- **Report Demands to `fraud_desk`:** Any demand for cash, commission, gifts, or OTPs—or any suspicious call, SMS link, or fake portal claiming to approve welfare benefits—must be reported immediately to the **`fraud_desk`** through:
  - The **MitraSeva Portal** (`Report Fraud / fraud_desk` module),
  - Direct complaint at any **District Collectorate Vigilance Counter**, or
  - Written submission citing the official's/intermediary's name, location, date, and Application Reference Number (ARN).

## 3 Verification of Official Communications

- Genuine status updates are issued exclusively under the citizen's official **Application Reference Number (ARN)** on the MitraSeva Citizen Portal or through official Block/District welfare offices.
- Citizens should verify any suspicious SMS or telephone call directly at a MitraKendra or Block Welfare Office before taking action.

## 4 Enforcement and Penalties

- Confirmed complaints logged with the `fraud_desk` trigger an independent inquiry within 7 working days.
- Public officials or contracted MitraKendra operators found soliciting fees face immediate suspension, contract blacklisting, and criminal prosecution under state anti-corruption statutes.
- Reporting a fraud attempt to the `fraud_desk` will never prejudice or delay the complainant's genuine welfare application.
