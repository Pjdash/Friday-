# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Rural Development and Housing
**Circular No.:** CIR-2026-06

## 1 Background
Applications for the current sanction cycle of **Gramin Awas Sahayata** (`SCH-HOU-01`) are entering their final phase. To ensure timely fund allocation and beneficiary verification prior to the winter construction window, the Department is issuing this formal reminder regarding the final closing date.

## 2 Amended Clause
All prospective applicants, Gram Panchayats, and Block Development Offices are hereby informed of the following binding deadline directive:

- **Strict Closing Date:** Applications for **Gramin Awas Sahayata** (`SCH-HOU-01`) close strictly on **2026-11-20**.
- **No Extensions:** No extensions will be granted under any circumstances beyond **2026-11-20**. The online submission portal and offline intake counters will automatically close at the end of **2026-11-20**, and late applications will not be entertained.

## 3 Effective Date
This circular takes effect on **2026-11-01**, with the strict scheme closing date enforced on **2026-11-20**.
