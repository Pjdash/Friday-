# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of e-Governance and Citizen Services
**Circular No.:** CIR-2026-10

## 1 Background
To improve access to public welfare services for daily-wage workers, farmers, students, and salaried residents unable to visit service centers on weekdays, the State Government has approved extended weekend operating hours at Jan Seva Kendras in three high-footfall jurisdictions.

## 2 Amended Clause
The operating schedule for public citizen facilitation centers is hereby updated as follows:

- **Extended Saturday Hours:** All **Nayagaon**, **Dakshinpur**, and **Thendralnagar** Jan Seva Kendras shall remain open on Saturdays from **10:00-14:00** (10:00 AM to 2:00 PM).
- **Services Available:** Full front-desk services—including scheme applications, document scanning and verification, status tracking, and grievance submission—shall be available during the Saturday **10:00-14:00** window.
- **Scope:** This directive applies exclusively to Jan Seva Kendras located within **Nayagaon**, **Dakshinpur**, and **Thendralnagar**; centers in other jurisdictions continue under standard schedules unless separately notified.

## 3 Effective Date
This circular takes effect on **2026-05-15**, with extended Saturday hours (10:00-14:00) operational from the first Saturday on or after **2026-05-15**.
