# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Labour and Employment
**Circular No.:** CIR-2026-04

## 1 Background
Following a state-level rationalization of worker welfare and insurance programs, the Government of Mitrapur Pradesh has decided to permanently discontinue new enrollments under the **Shramik Suraksha Bima** scheme (`SCH-INS-01`), previously governed by guideline `GL-INS-01`.

## 2 Amended Clause
In supersession of `GL-INS-01`, the following directive is issued:

- **Permanent Closure of New Enrollments:** **Shramik Suraksha Bima** (`SCH-INS-01`) is permanently closed for all new enrollments. No fresh applications, portal registrations, or Jan Seva Kendra submissions for new enrollment shall be accepted on or after **2026-10-01**.
- **Supersession:** Guideline `GL-INS-01` stands superseded for all new enrollment purposes. District labour offices and implementing agencies shall deactivate new enrollment links and display closure notices immediately.

## 3 Effective Date
This circular takes effect on **2026-10-01** and remains permanently in force.
