# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of School Education
**Circular No.:** CIR-2026-12

## 1 Background
Many applications for the **Balika Vidya Scholarship** (`SCH-EDU-01`) have been returned for correction due to incomplete or improperly formatted school bonafide certificates. To help students, parents, and school staff submit error-free documentation, the Department issues this plain-language Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) guide.

## 2 Amended Clause
The following plain-language FAQ governs the acceptance of school bonafide certificates under `SCH-EDU-01`:

1. **What must be printed on an acceptable school bonafide certificate?**
   It must be printed on the official school letterhead and clearly state the student's full name, father's or mother's name, current class/grade, academic year, admission/enrollment number, and the school's official UDISE or state recognition code.

2. **Who is authorized to sign the certificate?**
   The certificate must carry the handwritten signature of the **Principal or Headmaster** (or Acting Headmaster) along with the official round school stamp/seal and the date of issue. Certificates signed only by a class teacher or clerk will not be accepted.

3. **Can I submit a digital bonafide certificate from my school portal?**
   Yes, provided it is generated from a recognized school or state education portal and contains a verifiable QR code or reference verification number along with the school UDISE code.

4. **Is a fee receipt or school ID card accepted instead of a bonafide certificate?**
   No. Fee receipts, report cards, and student ID cards cannot replace a formal bonafide certificate.

5. **How recent must the bonafide certificate be?**
   The certificate must be issued for the **current academic session** and dated within the current academic year. Certificates from a previous class or prior academic year will be rejected.

6. **What if the student recently transferred to a new school?**
   Obtain the bonafide certificate from the **current school** where the student is enrolled. A transfer certificate from the previous school is not a substitute for a current bonafide certificate.

## 3 Effective Date
This circular takes effect on **2026-09-01** and applies to all **Balika Vidya Scholarship** (`SCH-EDU-01`) verifications conducted on or after **2026-09-01**.
