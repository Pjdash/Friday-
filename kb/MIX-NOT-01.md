# Urgent Notice: Balika Vidya Chhatravritti (Balika Vidya Scholarship)

**Mitrapur Pradesh Public SMS / WhatsApp Alert (`SCH-EDU-01` | `CIR-2026-02`)**

* **Last Date Extended:** Balika Vidya Chhatravritti (Balika Vidya Scholarship) apply karne ki aakhiri date ab **2026-11-21** hai (pehle 2026-10-31 thi, `CIR-2026-02` se extend hui).
* **Benefit:** Eligible female students ko **₹10,000** per year milte hain.
* **Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai:** Female student, age **22 saal ya usse kam**, aur annual household income **₹2,50,000 ya usse kam**.
* **Documents Ready Rakhein:** School/college bonafide certificate (`CIR-2026-12`), Aadhaar/age proof, income certificate, aur bank passbook.
* **Zero Fee (`POL-FEE-01`):** Application bilkul FREE (₹0) hai. Kisi ko bhi **₹500** ya koi bhi paisa na dein, aur apna **OTP** share na karein (`CIR-2026-09`). Fraud call aaye to **fraud_desk** par report karein.
* **Important:** Form submit karne se automatic approval nahi hota. Verification ke baad concerned department hi final decision leta hai.
