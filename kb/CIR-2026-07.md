# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
**Order / Amendment No.:** CIR-2026-07

## 1 Background
Field verification audits under scheme `SCH-FRM-01` identified inconsistent interpretations across tehsils regarding whether the `land_holding_acres` eligibility metric applies to individual agricultural plots or to the combined holdings of a farming household. This amendment issues a uniform binding rule for all revenue and agriculture officers.

## 2 Amended Clause
The land-holding computation clause under `SCH-FRM-01` is hereby clarified and amended as follows:

- **Household-Level Measurement:** The eligibility parameter `land_holding_acres` is measured **per farming household**, and **not per individual plot**.
- **Aggregation Rule:** All agricultural parcels owned or jointly held across different khata or survey numbers by members of the same farming household shall be aggregated to determine total `land_holding_acres`. Splitting applications by individual plot is not permitted.

## 3 Effective Date
This amendment takes effect on **2026-07-01** and applies to all verifications and determinations conducted on or after **2026-07-01**.
