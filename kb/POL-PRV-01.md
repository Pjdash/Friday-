# Citizen Data Privacy, Consent & Circular Precedence Policy (POL-PRV-01)

- **Issuing Authority:** Department of Welfare Governance & Citizen Services, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh
- **Applicability:** All welfare portals, MitraKendra Facilitation Centres, implementing departments, and official scheme documents of Mitrapur Pradesh.

## 1 Scope and Objective

This policy governs how citizen personal records are collected, verified, and protected during welfare scheme administration in Mitrapur Pradesh, and defines governance rules on document validity and official rule amendments.

## 2 Informed Consent and Purpose Limitation

- **Explicit Consent:** Implementing departments and MitraKendra operators must obtain the applicant's informed consent (digital or signed paper form) before collecting sensitive personal data, including household income, caste/community status, disability percentage, land records, and bank account details.
- **Strict Purpose Limitation:** Citizen data collected for a welfare application may be used solely for eligibility verification, duplicate-prevention checks, DBT benefit release, statutory audit, and grievance redressal. Sale, commercial sharing, or unauthorized disclosure of beneficiary lists is strictly prohibited.
- **Data Minimization:** Officers may only request documents marked **Mandatory** in the applicable scheme guideline. **Optional** documents cannot be demanded as a precondition for accepting an application.

## 3 Security, Correction Rights, and Retention

- **Credential Protection:** Citizen authentication records are encrypted in transit and at rest. In alignment with `POL-FEE-01`, staff and automated systems must never record or store a citizen's bank PIN or ask citizens to hand over OTPs over phone calls.
- **Right to Correction:** Beneficiaries may update bank account numbers, residential addresses, nominee details, or renewed certificates at any time free of charge through the MitraSeva Portal or a MitraKendra.
- **Retention:** Active beneficiary records are retained for the duration of scheme participation plus 5 financial years for audit compliance, after which non-statutory copies are securely purged.

## 4 Rule Precedence and Official Amendments

- **Base Guidelines and Circulars:** Initial scheme guidelines (`doc_type: guideline`) set out the baseline rules effective from their stated `effective_from` date.
- **Amendment Precedence:** Whenever an implementing department issues an official circular or notification with a later `effective_from` date or an explicit `supersedes` reference modifying income ceilings, application deadlines, or open/closed status, the **later official circular takes legal precedence** over the conflicting clause in the initial guideline, while all unmodified clauses of the base guideline remain in force.
