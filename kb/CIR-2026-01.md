# Government of Mitrapur Pradesh — Department of Social Welfare
**Order / Amendment No.:** CIR-2026-01

## 1 Background
Following a periodic review of household inflation and living costs across Mitrapur Pradesh, the Department of Social Welfare has approved an upward revision of the income ceiling governing eligibility under scheme `SCH-PEN-01` (guideline `GL-PEN-01`). This amendment also replaces the earlier interim clarification issued under `CIR-2026-11`, which ceased to be valid after 2026-09-09.

## 2 Amended Clause
In `GL-PEN-01`, **Section 3 (Income Eligibility Criteria)** is hereby amended as follows:

- **Existing Provision:** The maximum permissible `annual_household_income` for applicant households is ₹1,20,000.
- **Amended Provision:** The maximum permissible `annual_household_income` for applicant households is raised from **₹1,20,000** to **₹1,50,000**.

All income certificates submitted on or after the effective date shall be assessed against the revised ₹1,50,000 limit. This order supersedes `CIR-2026-11` in its entirety.

## 3 Effective Date
This amendment takes effect on **2026-09-10** and applies to all new applications and pending verifications processed on or after **2026-09-10**.
