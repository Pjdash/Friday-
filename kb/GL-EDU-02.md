# Yuva Kaushal Grant (SCH-EDU-02)

- **English:** Yuva Kaushal Grant
- **Hindi (Devanagari):** युवा कौशल अनुदान
- **Kannada:** ಯುವ ಕೌಶಲ್ಯ ಅನುದಾನ
- **Tamil:** இளைஞர் திறன் மானியம்
- **Implementing Department:** Directorate of Youth Skill Development, Government of Mitrapur Pradesh

## 1 Purpose

The Yuva Kaushal Grant equips students in Mitrapur Pradesh with industry-relevant vocational and digital skills by providing a direct training stipend. The grant reduces out-of-pocket expenses for certification materials, commuting, and lab practice during short-term accredited skill courses.

## 2 Benefit

- **Amount:** ₹8,000 one-time training stipend per eligible student.
- **Disbursement Mode:** Credited directly via DBT to the student's verified bank account in two stages: ₹4,000 upon verified course enrolment and ₹4,000 upon achieving at least 75% attendance and completing the accredited training module.

## 3 Eligibility

An applicant must meet all of the following conditions:

- **Residency:** Resident of Mitrapur Pradesh.
- **Occupation:** `occupation = student` (currently enrolled in a recognised college, university, ITI, polytechnic, or accredited training institution).
- **Age:** `18 <= age <= 28` years (applicant must be at least 18 years old and no older than 28 years as on the date of application).

## 4 Required Documents

- **Mitrapur Resident Identity Card** — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Must be active and unrevoked.
- **Age Proof Document** (Birth Certificate or Class 10 Board Certificate) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Permanent validity once issued by a recognised authority.
- **Student Status / Institutional ID or Bonafide Certificate** — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Must be valid for the current academic session and dated within 90 days of application.
- **Accredited Skill Course Admission Slip** (from a Mitrapur Skill Mission empanelled centre) — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Must be dated on or after `2026-12-15` and reflect an active upcoming batch.
- **Bank Account Proof (Passbook / Cancelled Cheque)** — **Mandatory.** *Validity note:* Active single-holder DBT-enabled account in the student's own name.
- **Prior Skill or Apprenticeship Certificate** — **Optional.** *Validity note:* Permanent validity; used only for advanced-track placement and does not restrict eligibility.

## 5 Application Process

1. Once applications open on `2026-12-15`, register on the **MitraSeva Citizen Portal** or apply through a **District Youth Skill Centre** or **MitraKendra**.
2. Upload student identity, age proof, and accredited course admission details to generate an **Application Reference Number (ARN)**.
3. The District Skill Coordinator verifies institutional enrolment and training batch accreditation within 15 working days.
4. **Final Decision:** Training centre admission and portal pre-validation do not guarantee stipend release. The **Directorate of Youth Skill Development makes the final decision** on applicant eligibility, grant sanction, and stipend disbursement.
5. No registration, portal, or stipend processing fee is charged under this scheme (see `POL-FEE-01`).
